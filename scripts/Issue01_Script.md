## What This Story Teaches (own page, framed, after the last story page)

Genesis begins by telling us who made everything: “In the beginning, God created the heavens and the earth” (Genesis 1:1). Again and again God speaks, and what he says happens. The Bible says the same thing later: “For he spoke, and it was done” (Psalm 33:9), and “By faith we understand that the universe has been framed by the word of God” (Hebrews 11:3). Each day God looked at what he had made and saw that it was good, and at the end, “it was very good” (Genesis 1:31). People were made in a special way: “In God’s image he created him” (Genesis 1:27). When God rested on the seventh day, he blessed it and made it holy, a pattern the Bible later gives to his people (Exodus 20:11). Psalm 19:1 puts it simply: “The heavens declare the glory of God.”
